# 🎈 POP Balloon

POP Balloon is a colorful incremental/clicker game where your goal is simple:

**Pop balloons, earn coins, buy upgrades, automate your damage, and work your way up to the Balloon King.**

The game combines active clicking with idle progression, equipment upgrades, special events, buffs and a growing progression system.

---

## 🎮 Features

- Multiple balloon tiers
- Click Damage progression
- Critical Hits
- Combo system
- Equipment and Auto DPS
- Global upgrades
- Coins and Diamonds
- Special balloons and temporary buffs
- Progressive unlock system
- Balloon King boss fight
- Endless Mode
- Save system
- Statistics
- Audio settings
- Cartoon-inspired UI and sound design

---

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Left Click** — Interact / Pop Balloon
- **ESC** — Pause / Back
- **Hold to Click** — Can be enabled in Settings
